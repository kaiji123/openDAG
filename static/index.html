An open dataset of causal DAGs from epidemiology papers

Paste the DOI or PMCID of an open-access observational or clinical study. NVIDIA Nemotron models on Nebius Token Factory extract its causal diagram and flag likely missing confounders; Tavily finds supporting evidence; you review and add it to the dataset.

  1. Fetching open-access full text from Europe PMC
  2. Extracting the DAG with Nemotron Nano
  3. Critiquing confounding & bias with Nemotron Super
  4. Finding evidence with Tavily

About OpenDAG

    
Causal diagrams (DAGs) make a study's assumptions explicit: what causes what, and which variables were
      adjusted for. Most papers never publish one. OpenDAG reconstructs them from open-access papers and builds
      an open, reusable dataset.

    
How it works

    
    
      
  1. Fetch: Europe PMC; only Creative Commons-licensed papers are accepted.
    2. 
      
  2. Extract:  (NVIDIA Nemotron Nano) reads the methods and returns exposure, outcome, adjusted covariates and edges as JSON.
    3. 
      
  3. Critique:  (larger Nemotron with reasoning) proposes unadjusted confounders and bias risks.
    4. 
      
  4. Evidence: Tavily searches for literature supporting each suggested confounder.
    5. 
      
  5. Validate: the graph is checked to be acyclic and schema-valid.
    6. 
      
  6. Review: a person edits, accepts or rejects suggestions, and approves.
    7. 
    

    
What we store

    
Bibliographic metadata, the DAG, AI critique, evidence links and short supporting phrases (≤20 words).
      Never the paper's full text.
